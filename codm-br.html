New BR Mode — Plunder: Treasure Heist

  • Enter the Anomalous Space.
  • Break through layers of Cognitive Reinforcements.
  • Secure the Treasure while managing rotations, respawns, and objective pressure.

New Weapon — FSS Hurricane

  • New SMG for Season 6 2026.
  • Designed around a larger magazine and stable handling.
  • Its trade-off is reduced range and raw stopping power.

Recent BR Systems Still Relevant

  • Rebirth Island was added in the previous major build.
  • Rebirth supports redeploys, contracts, Cash, Airdrops, and Buy Station Scorestreaks.
  • BR Killcam remains a testing feature and may vary by mode or region.

Caster Priority Checklist

  • Confirm the active mode before calling Gulag, redeploy, Cash, or Buy Station rules.
  • Track objective progress separately from eliminations in Treasure Heist.
  • Flag FSS Hurricane users as close-range pressure players rather than long-range anchors.
Mode-scope warning: Buy Stations, Gulag, infinite redeploys, Killcam, and Treasure Heist rules are not universal across every Classic BR lobby. Confirm the selected playlist and map before broadcast.

BR Systems — Mode-Specific Reference

Quick Reference
Buy Stations
Playlist dependent
  • Loot Cash on ground & from eliminations.
  • Spend at Buy Stations for Perks, Scorestreaks, weapons, armor/ammo refills, and special items.
  • Eliminated players drop Cash — third-party & scoop.
Gulag
Limited / playlist dependent
  • First death → 1v1 Gulag.
  • Win redeploys; lose needs buy tag at a Buy Station.
  • 1 free attempt; extras via Gulag Ticket.

Caster Callouts

  • Economy swing: Mention Cash thresholds before late-zone pushes.
  • Shop risk: “They’re shopping exposed”—build tension on 3rd-party angles.
  • Gulag state: Track who still has free attempt / Ticket and who requires buyback.

Other Details

Ammo & Weapons (quick)

  • Ammo types: Light • Heavy • Sniper • Shotgun • Rockets.
  • Ground loot: common → rare → epic → legendary. Airdrops grant stronger variants & mod slots.
  • Attachment mods: Extended, Long Barrel, Stabilizer, OWC Laser, Tactical Scope, etc.

  • Rebirth Island: redeploy, contracts, Cash, Airdrops, Buy Stations, and possible Killcam testing.
  • Classic Isolated / Blackout: do not assume event-only Gulag or Buy Station rules are active.
